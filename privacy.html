Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

n8n-brief is a personal automation used only by its owner, Matthew Polcer. It connects to one Google account: his own.

What it accesses

With permission granted through Google sign-in, n8n-brief reads messages in the owner's Gmail account and sends email from that account to the same account.

How the data is used

Email content is processed on the owner's own computer by self-hosted software (n8n and a local language model) to produce a daily summary and a job search report. The results are emailed to the owner only.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. It is not sent to any other person. If the owner turns on an optional review feature, a small number of selected messages may be sent to the Anthropic Claude API for a second opinion, under Anthropic's terms.

Storage

Data stays on the owner's computer. Google access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings under third-party access.

Google API Services

n8n-brief's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
